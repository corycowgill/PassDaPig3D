Scoring Guide

How to Play

  • Swipe up (or click Roll) to fling the pigs.
  • Your turn score adds up as you keep rolling.
  • Press Bank to add the turn score to your total and pass the pigs.
  • First to target score wins. Go over exactly and you win too.

Tips

  • A long fast swipe gives more spin and wilder rolls.
  • Push your luck — but a Pig Out costs your whole turn.
  • Use Bank to lock in safe points between rolls.
